# Item types he writes

This list was taken from his real practice booklets for grades 2-4 (2026). It records only the types and their usual stimulus, not his questions. His own files in `ornekler/` show the real items: read them for style, and never copy them.

A set of 15 mixes reading items with language-knowledge items. Not every item needs a reading passage. The stimulus can be a single sentence a child says, a word on a card, a note on the board, or a brochure. The context test still applies: the item must need its stimulus.

| Type | Typical stimulus | Typical stem |
| :- | :- | :- |
| Character, event, information | short story or tale (often shared by 2-3 items) | "Bu masalda anlatılan kişi hangisidir?" |
| Topic, main idea, main feeling | paragraph or short poem | "Bu metnin konusu nedir?", "Bu şiirin ana duygusu…" |
| Title | informative paragraph | "En uygun başlık hangisidir?" |
| Prediction from title or visuals | title plus picture, picture options | "Hangi görsel bu masala ait olabilir?" |
| What happens next / completing a text | story with a gap or "……" at the end | "Metin hangisiyle tamamlanabilir?" |
| Order of events | sentence or short text with a gap | "Hangi olay önce gerçekleşmiştir?" |
| Cause and effect | short text or sentence to complete | "…neden…", "sebep ve sonuç birlikte" |
| Problem and solution | short story about children | "Asıl problem nedir?", "En mantıklı çözüm…" |
| Real and imaginary | sentence to complete | "hayal ürünü bir ifade içermesi için" |
| Feelings | sentence or underlined line | "Hangi duygu vardır?" |
| Word meaning from context, idioms | sentence with an underlined word or phrase | "…sözüyle anlatılmak istenen…" |
| Synonyms and antonyms | a sentence with an underlined word, or numbered word pairs | "…zıt anlamlısı hangisinde kullanılmıştır?" |
| Word order, sentence building | word cards held by children | "Anlamlı ve kurallı cümle hangisidir?" |
| Spelling and capital letters | sentences in children's notebooks or on the board | "Hangi öğrenci yazım yanlışı yapmıştır?" |
| Punctuation | text with blanks "(   )", a rule box | "Hangi işaret kullanılmalıdır?" |
| Dialogue gap | two-child dialogue with "- - - -" | "Boş bırakılan yere hangisi getirilmelidir?" |
| Rules, brochures, notices | a short rules list or a brochure with an empty box | "Hangi davranış kurallara uygun değildir?" |
| Text types, information sources | a definition box, a word list for dictionary order | "Bilgilendirici bir metinde yer alabilir?" |

## Conventions seen in his sets

- **Shared stimulus:** a line in the form "1 ve 2. soruları yukarıdaki metne göre cevaplayınız." goes right after the stimulus.
- **Children as speakers:** "Hangi öğrenci…" items show children with speech bubbles or cards. These need a picture in the final booklet.
- **Short options in one row:** when the options are a word or two (A) biber B) limon C) karpuz), they sit in one row. Longer options go one per line.
- **Marked words:** underlined or bold words in the stimulus are referred to in the stem ("altı çizili sözcük").
