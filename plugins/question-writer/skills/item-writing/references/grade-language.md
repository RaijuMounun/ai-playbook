# Language for grades 2-4 (ages 7-10)

MEB's guide gives no numeric limits for this age group. The figures below are this plugin's own starting points, not MEB rules. When his past questions in `ornekler/` differ, they win.

| | Grade 2 (7-8) | Grade 3 (8-9) | Grade 4 (9-10) |
| :- | :- | :- | :- |
| Options | 3 (A-C) | 3 (A-C) | 4 (A-D) |
| Context length | about 30-80 words | about 50-120 words | about 70-150 words |
| Sentence length | mostly under 10 words | mostly under 12 words | mostly under 15 words |
| Stem | one short sentence | one sentence | one or two sentences |

The option counts come from him (2026-11-11). His `ayarlar.md` file can override them.

## Words and sentences

- **Vocabulary:** use everyday words a child of that age hears at home and at school. If a new word is the point of the item (finding its meaning from context), the context must make it guessable.
- **Sentence structure:** one idea per sentence. Avoid long chains of subordinate clauses in grade 2, and keep them rare in grades 3-4.
- **Names and settings:** use Turkish children's names and familiar places: school, family, the neighbourhood, nature, games, animals.
- **Topics to avoid:** brand names, frightening events, and sad topics (illness, death, accidents).
- **Spelling and punctuation:** follow TDK rules, including the circumflex where TDK uses it ("rüzgâr"). Use quotation marks consistently.
- **Visuals:** in grade 2, prefer pictures and pictographs to tables full of numbers (see the `visual-item` skill).
