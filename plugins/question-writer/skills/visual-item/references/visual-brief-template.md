# Visual brief for the publisher's illustrator

MEB gives no format for this. The template is this plugin's own, built on the guide's rule (section 2.5) that a visual must carry information the question needs.

The guide's example shows the difference:
- **Wrong:** the text says "three apples on the table" and the picture shows three apples. The picture repeats the text.
- **Right:** the visual shows something the text does not, such as how fast the apples rot, and the question needs it.

Write the brief in Turkish, under the item, with these headings:

```
GÖRSEL TARİFİ (Soru <n>)
Görsel türü: <resim / tablo / sütun grafiği / resim grafiği (piktogram) / afiş / harita / sıralı resimler>
Ne çizilecek: <the scene or the chart, in 2-4 sentences>
Mutlaka görünmesi gerekenler: <every detail, label and value the question depends on, with exact numbers and words>
Görünmemesi gerekenler: <anything that would give the answer away or add a second answer>
Yazılar: <every word that appears inside the visual, exactly as it must be written>
Boyut ve yerleşim: <next to the text / under the stem; text and visual on the same page>
Sorunun cevabı için gereken bilgi: <one sentence: what the student must read off the visual>
```

## Checks before handing it over

- **The visual is needed.** Cover the visual, and the question can no longer be answered. Cover the text, and the visual alone does not give away more than intended.
- **The values are reproducible.** Every number, label and colour that matters is written out, so the illustrator cannot change the answer by accident.
- **It is readable at the age.** There are few categories: about 3-5 in grade 2, and up to about 6 in grades 3-4. Labels are short and the colours are easy to tell apart. Answers never depend on colour alone.
- **One visual field.** The visual and the text it belongs to sit together, not on separate pages.
