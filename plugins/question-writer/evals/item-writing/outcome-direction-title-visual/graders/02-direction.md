The outcome is "T.O.3.2.b) Metnin başlığı ve görsellerinden hareketle metinde geçen bilgiler/olaylar hakkında tahminde bulunur": the student starts from a title and a picture and predicts what happens or what is told in the text.

The writer marks the answer key as the correct letter written three times on its own line under the stem (for example "CCC").

PASS only if all of these hold:
- The stimulus is a title together with a picture (a picture placeholder or description counts), and no full story text is given.
- The options are written predictions about the text's events or information (sentences), not pictures.
- The picture adds information the title alone does not give, so the answer needs both.
- There are exactly 3 options (A-C).

FAIL if the item gives a text and asks which picture or title fits it, if the options are pictures, or if the title alone gives the answer away.
