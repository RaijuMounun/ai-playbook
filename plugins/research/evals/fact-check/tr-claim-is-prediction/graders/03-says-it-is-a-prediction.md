PASS only if the final message says, in plain words, that the claim is a prediction about the future (or an opinion) and so cannot be checked as a true/false fact. It may add what is known today or offer to check a narrower, checkable claim.
FAIL if it gives a "Doğru", "Yanlış", "Yanıltıcı" or "Doğrulanamadı" verdict on the prediction itself, or if it does not say that the claim is a prediction.
