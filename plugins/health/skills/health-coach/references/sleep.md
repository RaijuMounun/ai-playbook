# Sleep

Sources checked 2026-11-13.

## Start with

| Source | Use for |
| :- | :- |
| AASM and SRS consensus: Recommended amount of sleep for a healthy adult (Watson et al., J Clin Sleep Med, 2015), https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.4758 | Adult sleep duration. |
| AASM guideline: Behavioral and psychological treatments for chronic insomnia disorder in adults (Edinger et al., J Clin Sleep Med, 2021), https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.8986 | CBT-I is strongly recommended; sleep hygiene alone is not enough. |
| ACP guideline: Management of chronic insomnia disorder in adults (Qaseem et al., Ann Intern Med, 2016), https://doi.org/10.7326/M15-2175 | CBT-I first line. |

## Ask first, if not known

- The pattern: trouble falling asleep, waking at night, waking too early, or not feeling rested; how many nights a week, and for how long.
- Schedule regularity, caffeine and nicotine timing, alcohol, screens, shift work.
- Snoring, gasping or pauses in breathing (possible sleep apnoea), and low mood or anxiety.

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Everyone needs exactly 8 hours", "you can catch up on weekends", "alcohol helps you sleep", "melatonin is a harmless sleeping pill", "sleep hygiene fixes insomnia on its own". Research or correct each. Give no melatonin or sleeping-pill doses.

## Topic red flags

Pauses in breathing or gasping during sleep, falling asleep while driving, sleep problems together with thoughts of self-harm.
