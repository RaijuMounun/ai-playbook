# Hair and hair loss

Sources checked 2026-11-13.

## Start with

| Source | Use for |
| :- | :- |
| EDF/EADV evidence-based (S3) guideline for the treatment of androgenetic alopecia in women and men (JEADV, 2018), https://doi.org/10.1111/jdv.14624 | Main European guideline: finasteride, topical minoxidil and other treatments, with evidence grades. No newer S3 version found; check for one. |
| EMA Article 31 referral on finasteride- and dutasteride-containing medicines (2025), https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/referrals/finasteride-dutasteride-containing-medicinal-products | Current safety position: suicidal ideation added as a side effect of finasteride tablets (EC decision, Aug 2025); users with mood changes are told to stop and seek advice. Benefit still judged to outweigh risk. |
| NHS, Hair loss (reviewed 2024), https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/hair-loss/ | Plain-language overview of causes and treatment options. Not for side effects. |

Not found at guideline level (research it per question, and label the evidence honestly): low-dose oral minoxidil, ketoconazole shampoo for hair loss, persistent sexual effects after finasteride ("post-finasteride syndrome").

## Ask first, if not known

- Pattern: receding temples or thinning crown (androgenetic) vs diffuse shedding (telogen effluvium after illness, stress, weight loss) vs round bald patches (alopecia areata).
- Family history, how long it has been going on, and whether a dermatologist has diagnosed it.
- What has been tried, and why it was stopped (cost, daily effort and side effects decide which option is realistic).
- Mood history and plans for a sexual relationship, when finasteride is on the table (both change the side-effect discussion).

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Sulfate or paraben shampoo causes hair loss", "hair loss means high testosterone", "frequent washing causes hair loss", "a hair transplant makes medication unnecessary", "biotin regrows hair". Research or correct each. Treatment choice and dose are the dermatologist's decision.

## Topic red flags

Round bald patches, a painful, scaly or scarring scalp, sudden shedding in clumps, or hair loss with other symptoms (weight change, fatigue): see a doctor. On finasteride, low mood or thoughts of self-harm: stop and seek advice the same day (EMA 2025); thoughts of self-harm with a plan go to 112.
