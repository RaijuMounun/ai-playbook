## Ask first, if not known

- What the problem looks like, where, and for how long; what has been tried.
- Skin type, products used, sun exposure, shaving.
- Medications (some cause acne or sun sensitivity), and whether a dermatologist is already involved.

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Toothpaste dries out pimples", "detox skincare", "a base tan protects from sunburn", "oily skin doesn't need moisturiser", "chocolate causes acne", "natural products are safer". Research or correct each. Prescription treatments (isotretinoin, antibiotics) are a dermatologist's decision: explain them, never dose them.

## Topic red flags

A mole that changes, bleeds or itches; a fast-spreading red, hot, painful area with fever; a widespread rash with fever or blistering; swelling of the face or lips.
