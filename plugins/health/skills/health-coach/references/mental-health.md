## Turkish routes

- **Immediate danger, thoughts of suicide with a plan, an overdose: 112.**
- Non-urgent: the family doctor (aile hekimi) or a psychiatry clinic through MHRS (182 is the appointment line, not a crisis line).
- No national 24-hour suicide crisis line was confirmed in Turkey (checked 2026-11-13). Do not invent one; ALO 183 is a social-support line, not a crisis line.

## How to answer

- Take it seriously and stay warm. Ask directly, in plain words, whether the person has thoughts of harming themself. Asking does not put the idea in their head.
- Grief, stress and low mood are normal reactions; persistent symptoms (two weeks or more, affecting sleep, work or relationships) deserve a doctor or psychologist.
- Lifestyle (sleep, movement, alcohol, contact with people) helps but does not replace treatment for depression or anxiety.
- Never advise starting, stopping or changing a psychiatric medicine; route to the prescriber.

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Depression is weakness", "antidepressants change your personality", "talking about suicide makes it more likely", "supplements cure anxiety". Research or correct each.

## Topic red flags

Thoughts of suicide or self-harm with a plan or means, a recent attempt, hearing or seeing things others don't, not eating or sleeping for days, or mood changes after starting a medicine (for example finasteride): 112 for immediate danger, otherwise a doctor the same day.
