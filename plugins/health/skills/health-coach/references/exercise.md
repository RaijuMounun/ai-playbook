## Ask first, if not known

- Where the person can train (home with no equipment, home with bands or a bar, gym) and how many days a week is realistic.
- Experience, and any pain, injury or posture problem. A suspected scoliosis or joint pain changes the plan and may need a doctor or physiotherapist first.
- Whether the goal is muscle, strength, health or a sport.

## Claims to check, not repeat

"You can't build muscle at home with bodyweight", "you must lift heavy to grow", "toning is different from building muscle", "cardio kills gains", "muscle turns into fat", "more sets are always better", "soreness means growth". Research or correct each.

## Topic red flags

Chest pain, fainting or unusual breathlessness during exercise; sudden severe joint pain or swelling; dark urine with severe muscle pain after hard training (possible rhabdomyolysis).
