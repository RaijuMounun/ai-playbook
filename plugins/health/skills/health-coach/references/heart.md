## How to answer

- Family history matters by who and at what age: a first-degree relative (parent, sibling, child) with early heart disease or an aortic dissection or aneurysm weighs more than a grandparent. Say which relation it is.
- Aortic disease in a relative: whether relatives need imaging depends on the type (thoracic vs abdominal, genetic syndrome or not). This is a cardiologist's decision; research the ESC 2024 recommendation for the specific case.
- Risk is the sum of factors: age, sex, smoking, blood pressure, LDL, diabetes, family history. Use the profile and its labs.

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Normal cholesterol means no heart risk", "young people can't have heart problems", "a heart check-up machine test rules out everything", "supplements lower cholesterol as well as medicine". Research or correct each.

## Topic red flags

Chest pain or pressure, sudden tearing pain in the chest, back or abdomen (possible aortic dissection), fainting, or sudden breathlessness: 112 now.
