## Ask first, if not known

- The goal: gain weight or muscle, lose fat, or a condition such as high LDL or blood sugar.
- Budget, cooking access, and how regular meals are. The person's real constraints decide what is doable.
- Allergies, a vegetarian or religious diet, any diagnosed condition or medication.
- Signs of an eating disorder: if present, give no calorie or weight targets (see red-flags.md).

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Protein must be eaten within 30 minutes after training", "eating late makes you fat", "detox teas or juices clean the liver", "fruit sugar is as bad as added sugar", "everyone needs supplements", "gaining weight means eating junk food". Research or correct each; don't build on it.

## Topic red flags

Unexplained weight loss, trouble swallowing, vomiting blood or black stool, signs of an eating disorder.
