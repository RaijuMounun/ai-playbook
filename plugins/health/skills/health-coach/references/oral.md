## Ask first, if not known

- Brushing routine (how often, when), flossing, the toothpaste's fluoride content.
- Coffee, tea, smoking (staining and gum disease), sugary drinks.
- Pain, sensitivity, bleeding gums, and the last dentist visit.

## Claims to check, not repeat

"Brush right after acidic food or drinks", "harder brushing cleans better", "charcoal toothpaste whitens safely", "whitening toothpaste removes deep stains", "fluoride in toothpaste is dangerous". Research or correct each.

## Topic red flags

Facial or jaw swelling with fever, trouble swallowing or opening the mouth, a mouth sore that has not healed in 2–3 weeks.
