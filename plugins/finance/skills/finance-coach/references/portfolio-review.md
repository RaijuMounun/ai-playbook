# Portfolio review

"Portföyümü değerlendir", or once a year as the policy says, or when a maturity or a large sum comes up.

1. Read `Portfoy.md` and `Yatirim_Politikasi.md`. Ask for current amounts if the note is old (more than a month).
2. Fetch current fund data for each holding (one researcher for the whole portfolio).
3. Show the current allocation next to the policy's target and bands.
4. Check:
   - **Drift:** a class outside its band.
   - **Fees:** any holding above the policy's fee limit, or much above funds doing the same job.
   - **Overlap and concentration:** the same stocks across funds; one fund above the policy's single-fund limit.
   - **Emergency fund:** still covered in a liquid place.
   - **Dates:** maturities and reviews in the next 30 days.
   - **Real return** of each holding since purchase where the user gave buy dates and prices.
5. Verdict: what is in line with the policy, and what is not. Rebalancing follows the policy's own rule (yearly or on a band breach); show which class is over and which is under, in TL. New money first goes to the underweight class, which avoids selling and its tax.
6. Offer to update `Portfoy.md` and add the next review date to `Takip.md`.
