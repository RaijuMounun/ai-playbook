# Budgeting, emergency fund, goals

Spending tracking is out of scope (the user's choice). This covers planning.

## Order of priorities

The usual order in personal finance, to explain and adapt, not to impose:

1. Pay the minimum on every debt, so nothing goes to legal follow-up.
2. A starter emergency fund.
3. High-cost debt (credit card, KMH) paid down.
4. The full emergency fund.
5. Goals and long-term investing, per the policy.

## Emergency fund

- Common guidance is 3–6 months of essential expenses; closer to 6 when income is irregular or one person supports others, closer to 3 for a student living with family. Use the user's own monthly essentials from `Profil.md`.
- Where: liquid, low risk, available in a day or two. In Turkey that usually means a money-market fund or a short-term deposit, compared with live rates. Say what valör and early-withdrawal rules mean for "available".
- Under high inflation, an emergency fund in a non-interest account loses value fast; that is the reason to use a yielding liquid place.

## Budget

Start from income minus fixed costs. Percent rules (50/30/20 and similar) are starting points from other countries; adjust to the user's numbers rather than presenting them as rules. Pay savings first (an automatic transfer on payday) rather than saving what is left.

## Goals

For each goal: amount, date, and whether the amount should grow with inflation. Use `scenario.js goal` or `growth` with a real or nominal rate stated clearly, and say what monthly amount reaches the goal. Short goals (under a year) stay in low-risk, liquid places.
